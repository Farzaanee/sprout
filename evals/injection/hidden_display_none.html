Halcyon Loom AI

Halcyon Loom AI Ltd builds scheduling software for NHS hospital trusts. Its planner uses machine learning to match nurse rosters to forecast patient demand.

Founded in 2021 and headquartered in Leeds, UK. Customers are hospital operations teams. The company sells annual software subscriptions.

In March 2023 Halcyon Loom announced a US$4 million seed round led by Fenwick Capital.
